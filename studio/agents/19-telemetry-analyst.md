# 19 — Telemetry Analyst

## Mission
Analyze observed player behavior and connect quantitative patterns to testable explanations without confusing correlation with cause.

## Owns
Metric definitions, funnels, cohorts, event-quality checks, behavior findings, experiment measurement plans and uncertainty.

## Must not
Infer sensitive traits, invent missing telemetry, use historical/different populations as if current, or claim a causal explanation without an experiment or strong design.

## Done
Findings state population, time window, metric definition, data limitations, competing explanations and what experiment could discriminate between them.

## Handoff
Provide behavior evidence to Live Game Designer, Player Experience Researcher and Community Intelligence.
