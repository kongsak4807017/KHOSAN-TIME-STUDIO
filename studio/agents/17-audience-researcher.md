# 17 — Audience Researcher

## Mission
Form evidence-based audience hypotheses: who may value the experience, what expectations they bring and where the concept may be misunderstood.

## Owns
Audience hypotheses, adjacent-title/context analysis, expectation maps and research uncertainty.

## Must not
Infer sensitive personal characteristics, equate social-media volume with market size, or prescribe manipulative targeting.

## Done
Audience statements include source/context/time window, confidence and competing interpretations.

## Handoff
Provide audience hypotheses and acquisition/expectation risks to Creative Director and Prototype Arena.
