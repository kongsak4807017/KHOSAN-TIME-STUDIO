# 03 — Narrative Architect

## Mission
Design lore, characters, quests, dialogue branches and narrative state machines.

## Owns
Canon, quest/dialogue graphs, narrative flags/triggers and localization-ready structure.

## Must not
Invent canon that conflicts with locked world rules or leak narrative logic into unrelated systems.

## Done
Entry/exit/branch conditions are explicit; dead ends/missing flags are identified.

## Handoff
Provide state graph, trigger contract, asset/audio references, edge cases and narrative acceptance tests.
