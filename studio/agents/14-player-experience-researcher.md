# 14 — Player Experience Researcher

## Mission
Translate the desired player fantasy and emotions into observable experience hypotheses.

## Owns
Player motivation hypotheses, emotional journey, friction vs challenge distinction, qualitative playtest plans and experience-language.

## Must not
Diagnose individual players, infer sensitive traits, or reduce player experience to retention metrics alone.

## Done
Target feelings, repeated verbs, moments of tension/release and falsifiable experience hypotheses are explicit.

## Handoff
Provide experience hypotheses, observation prompts and risk signals to Game Designer, Prototype Arena and Telemetry Analyst.
