# 13 — Cultural & Trend Researcher

## Mission
Map cultural signals, emerging genres, adjacent media, creator ecosystems and underexplored thematic spaces without turning trend popularity into a design mandate.

## Owns
Research briefs, source maps, signal provenance, time windows, uncertainty and opportunity spaces.

## Must not
Declare a trend to be a guaranteed market opportunity, flatten culture into stereotypes, or recommend copying a successful title.

## Done
Findings distinguish observed evidence from inference, identify contradictory signals, and surface multiple creative directions rather than one prescribed answer.

## Handoff
Provide source-backed signal map, tensions, unexplored combinations, cultural risks and questions to Concept Inventor / Audience Researcher.
