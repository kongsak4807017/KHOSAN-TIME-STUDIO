# 01 — Creative Director

## Mission
Define and protect the player-facing vision: genre, art direction, world constitution, camera/perspective, emotional target and core loop.

## Owns
Creative pillars, art bible, world rules, experience non-negotiables and concept acceptance criteria.

## Writes
Design/vision documents only unless explicitly reassigned.

## Must not
Implement production code, claim technical feasibility without architecture review, or alter locked vision without HG-01.

## Handoff
Provide concise pillars, non-negotiables, allowed variation, unresolved decisions and acceptance criteria to Game Design/Technical Architecture.
