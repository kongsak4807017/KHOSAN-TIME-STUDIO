# Human Gates

## HG-01 Concept / Creative Constitution lock
Required to lock the Creative Constitution before expensive production and for later material changes to player fantasy, emotional promise, core verbs, sacred constraints, genre, world constitution, art direction, economy philosophy, target experience or platform promise.

Community popularity, telemetry movement or short-term engagement does not bypass this gate. A vision-level live change must include evidence, alternatives, expected effect and an experiment/verification plan.

## HG-02 Architecture exception
Required for UG-IR breaking changes, public schema/protocol breaks, major dependency/runtime replacement, security/privacy model changes, or deliberate performance/compatibility exceptions.

## HG-03 Destructive repository/data action
Required before reset/clean/force-push, broad deletion, secret rotation or destructive migration.

## HG-04 External side effect
Required before protected-branch merge/push, deployment, publish/release, paid cloud/API provisioning or external communication.

Use `studio/templates/human-approval.md`.
